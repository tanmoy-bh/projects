Strategy · Reliability

Engineering Strategy

An engineering strategy concept for a carsharing platform. Track every step of a trip from unlock to return, ship app releases in stages that halt themselves when unlock success drops, and let AI draft incidents for a person to approve. Friction points come from public app reviews; every metric is sample data.
